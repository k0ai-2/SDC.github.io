Meet The Team

TEAM INTRODUCTION

Serdang Drum Community, based in Selangor, is one of Malaysia's 24 Festive Drums teams. It was founded by a group of passionate alumni from SMK Seri Kembangan who share a deep love for the art of drumming. Our members come from diverse backgrounds—students, teachers, pharmacists, engineers, agency professionals, makeup artists, and entrepreneurs. Although we play different roles in our careers, we hold equally important responsibilities within the team.

Since our establishment, we have used the sound of the drums to promote unity and the rhythm to strengthen our community. The team has completed more than 50 performances in school and over 30 external shows, including concerts, commercial events, charity events, and community celebrations. In 2018 and 2023, we successfully held two major productions—Our Drum Stories and Our Drum Story 2—both receiving overwhelming support with full-house audiences. We are now actively preparing for Our Drum Story 3 in 2027.

The Drum Tradition We Perform

Gendang 24 Perayaan (Gendang 24 Musim) — the Malay name for our Malaysian Chinese drum performance tradition.

24 Festive Drums (24 Season Drums) — the English name for the same tradition.

Our Story

BORN FROM THE BEAT

Serdang Drum Club started with a circle of passionate drummers who believed rhythm could bring people together. From backyard practice sessions to full-stage performances, we've grown into one of Selangor's most energetic drum performance groups.

We perform at corporate events, festivals, cultural celebrations, and private functions - bringing high-energy drum shows that leave audiences speechless.
